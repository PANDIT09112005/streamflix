# 🎬 StreamFlix – Movie Streaming Platform

## 📖 About the Project

StreamFlix is a modern movie and TV show streaming platform designed to provide users with an engaging and seamless entertainment experience. The platform offers an intuitive interface where users can explore movies, discover TV shows, browse content by categories, and enjoy a visually appealing streaming-style experience.

The project focuses on creating a responsive, user-friendly interface inspired by modern OTT platforms, making content discovery simple, interactive, and enjoyable.

## ✨ Features

- 🎥 **Movie Discovery** – Explore a wide collection of movies and entertainment content.
- 📺 **TV Shows** – Browse TV shows and series through an organized interface.
- 🔍 **Search Functionality** – Easily find movies and shows.
- 🎭 **Genre-Based Browsing** – Discover content based on different genres and categories.
- 🖼️ **Dynamic Movie Cards** – Display movie posters, titles, and details in an attractive layout.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Optimized for different screen sizes and devices.
- 🎨 **Modern UI** – Clean design with a streaming-platform-inspired interface.
- ⭐ **Content Details** – Explore information about movies and shows.
